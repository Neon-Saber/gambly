this is what you can't do with {{ bot_name }} or the dashboard. it applies to players, staff and the server owner.

Playing

  • don't sell, buy or trade chips, items or accounts for real money, crypto, gift cards, game items or anything else of value.
  • don't use bugs or exploits to get chips. report them in a ticket instead.
  • don't use self-bots, macros, scripts or alt accounts to farm chips, quests or XP.
  • don't spam commands to get around cooldowns or slow the bot down.

Reports, tickets and the people around you

  • don't use tickets, reports or the Report Message action to harass people or flood staff with fake reports.
  • don't put other people's personal information in game names, tickets or reports.
  • follow the rules of the server and Discord's Community Guidelines.

Staff

  • use staff commands and the dashboard for running the server, not for settling personal fights.
  • don't hand out chips, ranks or roles to yourself or friends to get ahead in the game.
  • don't try to get around your rank's limits, for example by asking someone higher to run commands you aren't allowed to.

The dashboard and the bot itself

  • don't try to get into servers or data you don't have access to.
  • don't attack, overload, scrape or automate the dashboard or the bot.
  • don't try to copy or impersonate {{ bot_name }} to trick people.

What happens if you do

depending on what happened, staff can warn you, change or reset your chips, block you from the games, or remove you from the server. serious abuse can be reported to Discord.
