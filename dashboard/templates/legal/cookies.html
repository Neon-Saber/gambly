this dashboard sets one cookie, and only after you sign in. there are no analytics, ads or tracking cookies, so there's no cookie banner.

sessionkeeps you logged in. it only holds a random ID; your actual session (Discord profile, server list, sign-in token) is stored on the dashboard server. it's marked HttpOnly (page scripts can't read it), SameSite=Lax, and Secure on the live site7 days, or until you log out

Things that aren't cookies

  • the fonts load from Google Fonts. Google doesn't set cookies for this, but your browser does send it your IP address. see the Privacy Policy.
  • the dashboard doesn't use local storage or other tracking in your browser.

Turning it off

you can block cookies in your browser, but then you can't stay signed in. the public pages (help and these policies) work without it.
