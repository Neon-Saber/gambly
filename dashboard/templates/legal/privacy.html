this covers what the {{ bot_name }} bot and this dashboard collect, why, where it's kept and how to get rid of it. {{ bot_name }} is run by {{ who }}, who decides how this data is used.

What the bot stores

datawhy
your Discord user ID and display nameto know whose balance, level or warning is whose, and to show names on leaderboards
game data: wallet, bank, loans, items, business, upgrades, badges, streaks, quests, collection, rebirths and cooldown timesso the game remembers your progress
level and XPfor the leveling system
moderation warnings: the reason, who gave it and whenso staff can see someone's history
an activity log of notable events (loans, bans, shop buys, staff and dashboard changes), which can include display names. the newest 500 entries are keptso the server owner can see what happened
server settings chosen by staff (channels, roles, which games are on)to run the server's features

if you use the personal app (the bot added to your own account), your game data is kept in one shared personal ledger that's separate from any server's.

What the bot reads but doesn't keep

  • message content, to recognise ! commands and to give XP for chatting. it isn't saved.
  • if staff turn on message logs, edits and deletions are posted to a private staff channel inside Discord. ticket transcripts are uploaded to a staff channel in Discord when a ticket closes. neither is stored outside Discord by the bot.
  • server members and roles, for welcome messages, the member counter, finding people for commands, staff ranks and handing out roles. avatars are shown in some embeds.

What the dashboard stores

  • when you sign in, Discord gives us your user ID, username, display name, avatar and the list of servers you're in with your permissions there. we ask for the identify and guilds scopes only.
  • that, plus the sign-in token Discord gives us, is kept in a server-side session for up to 7 days or until you log out. see the Cookie Policy.
  • changes you make in the dashboard are written to the activity log with your username.

data may be stored in {{ ph('regions the Upstash, Oracle and Render services are set to') }}. we don't sell your data, use it for ads, share it with anyone else, or use it to train AI.

How long it's kept

game data and levels stay until you delete them, staff reset them, or the operator removes them. warnings stay until staff clear them, even if you delete your other data. login sessions end after 7 days or when you log out. the activity log only keeps its newest 500 lines.

Your choices

  • see and download everything tied to your Discord ID from your account.
  • delete it from your account page (every server at once), or with /deletemydata in Discord (just the server or ledger you run it in). both remove your game data and level and can't be undone. moderation records are kept: warnings, and staff actions already posted to the server's log channels, stay so staff can still enforce the rules. if you think a warning is wrong, open a ticket and staff can review it. lines in the activity log aren't removed this way either. ask us and we'll remove those.
  • stop the dashboard session by logging out, or remove the app's access under Discord → User Settings → Authorized Apps.

depending on where you live you may have more rights, like correcting your data or complaining to a regulator. contact us below and we'll help.

Kids

{{ bot_name }} is only for people old enough to use Discord under Discord's own rules. we don't knowingly collect anything extra from children.

Security

the dashboard runs over HTTPS, sign-in tokens stay on the server instead of in your browser, and the session cookie can't be read by page scripts. no system is perfect, so don't put anything sensitive in game names, tickets or reports.

Changes

if what we collect changes, this page changes first and the date at the top updates.
